# Daily Operations Checklist for Store Owner & Staff

Keep this checklist handy at the showroom front desk to ensure smooth daily operations and fast customer order fulfillment.

---

## 🌅 Morning Routine (10:00 AM – 11:30 AM)

- [ ] **Check Overnight Web Orders**:
  1. Open `https://laptoppointbd.com/admin/orders`.
  2. Filter by status: **Pending**.
  3. Call each customer on their mobile number to verify:
     - Customer name and delivery address (district, thana/area).
     - Confirmed laptop model and pricing.
     - Courier preference (Cash on Delivery / Showroom Pickup).
  4. Once confirmed by phone, change order status to **Confirmed**.

- [ ] **Review Contact Form Messages**:
  1. Open **Admin → Messages** (`/admin/messages`).
  2. Inspect any new inquiries from the website contact page.
  3. Reply via phone call or WhatsApp for hot sales leads.
  4. Click **Mark Handled** once answered.

- [ ] **Review Customer Product Q&A**:
  1. Open **Admin → Questions** (`/admin/questions`).
  2. Check for customer inquiries on specific laptop pages.
  3. Write a polite, accurate response and click **Publish Answer**.

---

## 📦 Midday Order Dispatch Routine (2:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

- [ ] **Inspect & Package Hardware**:
  - Run hardware diagnostic (battery health, keyboard keys, display pixels, ports).
  - Clean laptop exterior and screen.
  - Include original/compatible power charger and power cable.
  - Include warranty card / serial memo.

- [ ] **Print Paperwork**:
  1. Open the order in **Admin → Orders**.
  2. Click the **Print** icon.
  3. Print the **A4 Tax Invoice** (place inside the box).
  4. Print the **A4 Delivery Challan** (attach to outside of courier parcel).

- [ ] **Dispatch to Courier & Input Tracking**:
  1. Hand parcels to courier representative (Steadfast, RedX, Pathao, Sundarban).
  2. Receive courier consignment / tracking code.
  3. Open the order in **Admin → Orders**, update status to **Shipped**, enter the tracking number, and save.
  4. *(The customer can now track their shipment in real time at `https://laptoppointbd.com/track-order`)*.

---

## 🌙 Evening Closing Routine (7:30 PM – 8:30 PM)

- [ ] **Synchronize Showroom Walk-in Sales with Website Stock**:
  - If a laptop was sold directly over the counter in the showroom, open **Admin → Products** and reduce its stock via the **Quick Stock Dialog** (or mark it **0** if it was the last unit).
  - *Never leave a sold-out showroom laptop listed as available online to prevent double-selling.*

- [ ] **Check Cancellations or Returns**:
  - If a courier parcel was refused or returned, change order status to **Cancelled** or **Returned**.
  - Confirm the system restored the stock to your inventory.

- [ ] **Security Check**:
  - Log out of the admin console if using a shared showroom computer.
